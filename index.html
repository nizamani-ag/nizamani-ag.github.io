About

I am a PhD candidate at Østfold University of Applied Sciences, Norway, working at the intersection of climate change and urban sustainability. My research combines environmental sensing, nature-based solutions, and physics-informed machine learning to understand and mitigate urban heat and air pollution. I am open to postdoctoral opportunities in urban climate, green infrastructure, and machine learning applications in environmental science.

Research Interests

  • Urban climate and heat island mitigation
  • Nature-based solutions and green infrastructure
  • Air quality monitoring and low-cost mobile sensors
  • Physics-informed neural networks for urban environmental modelling
  • Urban resilience to climate extremes
