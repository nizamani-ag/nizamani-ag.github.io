Urban Climate Adaptation and Resilient Development

My research examines how urban form, infrastructure, and nature-based solutions shape climate risk, and how cities can develop while reducing long-term vulnerability.

Research

01

Nature-Based Solutions and Urban Adaptation

Nature-based interventions — green roofs, vertical greenery, and related systems — for stormwater management, heat mitigation, and climate adaptation.

02

Computational Urban Climate and Resilience

Computational and physics-informed modelling, remote sensing, and spatial analysis to understand urban climate and test adaptation interventions.

03

Urban Development, Infrastructure and Climate Risk

How urban form, infrastructure decisions, institutions, and development patterns interact with climate hazards to shape long-term resilience and vulnerability.
