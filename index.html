About

I am a Doctoral Research Fellow at Østfold University of Applied Sciences, Norway, working at the intersection of climate change and urban sustainability. My PhD thesis, "Green Sponge Buildings" (supervised by Geir Torgersen), explores how buildings can act as climate-resilient sponges through green infrastructure and nature-based solutions. My research combines environmental sensing, urban stormwater management, and physics-informed machine learning to mitigate urban heat and pollution.

I am open to postdoctoral opportunities in urban climate, green infrastructure, and machine learning applications in environmental science.

Research Interests

  • Urban climate and heat island mitigation
  • Nature-based solutions and green infrastructure
  • Physics-informed neural networks for urban environmental modelling
  • Urban resilience to climate extremes
